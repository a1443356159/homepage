We introduce a training-free looping framework that adds inference computation
inside frozen flow-matching image denoisers. Dense and Sparse Token Loop,
Sampling-Progress Gating, and Loop Guidance provide explicit control over which
tokens, layers, and sampling times receive repeated computation.
