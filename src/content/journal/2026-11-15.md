title: "Inspired by BLG"
date: 2026-11-15
---

I was too excited to fall asleep until around 2:30 a.m. A mosquito woke me at 5:30, so I got up and watched BLG win the three crucial games against Gen.G. I was so happy that I cried during Bin’s speech. To me, he seemed to have let go of the pressure of being the world’s best top laner while still playing like one. I was also impressed by Xia An’s fluent interpretation between Chinese, English, and Korean.

It left me feeling inspired. Maybe I should work toward becoming the world’s best programmer too, haha.

I went to get my laptop repaired at an official service shop in the morning. After the repair, it was no longer lagging, although I still noticed some throttling. Later, I reviewed Theory of Computation and Numerical Analysis and worked on a short video. Tonight I want to make some progress on my loop transformer research. I’ve spent a lot of time playing during Worlds; it’s time to get serious about studying again.
