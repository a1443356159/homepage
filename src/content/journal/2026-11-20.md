The Numerical Analysis quiz this morning was tricky. It took me a while to find an approach. I spent much of the rest of the day reviewing decidability and NP-completeness, and both were tough going.

In the evening, I gave a talk called “Computing Power: The Foundation of AI” to the supercomputing team. I compared desktop and data-center GPUs with phone and edge accelerators. Peak compute only tells part of the story: memory capacity, bandwidth, and the software stack also shape how well a model runs.

I also got some preliminary results from running a folded model on-device. It saved space, but inference was slower. So far, the savings don’t justify the slowdown.
