Today I tried to figure out why my laptop had become slower after cleaning and replacing the thermal paste. Even everyday tasks were lagging, although the fans were running normally.

I looked into the CPU’s thermal throttling reports and asked for help narrowing down the cause. At the time, I was still trying to narrow down the cause.


Looking back, this started with a spur-of-the-moment decision to replace the thermal paste myself. I got the procedure and the type of paste wrong, which led to the slowdown.
