---
title: "Quantizing a looped Transformer"
date: 2026-11-27
---

I went back through my research meeting notes and pulled out the main points and next steps for on-device inference. One question kept coming up for me: when a model reuses the same weights, how much of the quantization error carries into the next pass?

I looked at [LoopQuant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16368) for a different angle on that question. The weights are shared, but the activations change from pass to pass. That makes choosing the right precision for each pass part of the problem.
