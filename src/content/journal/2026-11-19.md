date: 2026-11-19

I shared an update on my Loop Transformer experiments. On a phone, I compared looping through the whole stack with repeating each layer before moving on. In the layer-wise setup, I tried loading the next layer’s weights ahead of time. I’m still figuring out what limits inference speed.

I also came across *Folding Language Models into Themselves*. It made me wonder whether sharing weights between adjacent layers could cut memory traffic enough to speed up inference on a phone. Next I want to measure weight-loading time separately from scheduling overhead and test that idea. I’d also like to see whether one accelerator can fill in missing attention-cache entries after an early exit while another keeps generating.
