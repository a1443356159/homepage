title: "Reading about looped inference and revisiting 2-SAT"
date: 2026-11-11
---

I read a couple of papers on looped transformers. I wanted to understand how repeating the same layers affects inference time, where the serving overhead comes from, and what can run in parallel.

For Theory of Computation, I went back over how to find a satisfying assignment for 2-SAT using an implication graph. I also worked through the reduction from 3-SAT to Max-2-SAT as part of my review of NP-completeness.
