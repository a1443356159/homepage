title: "What looped models carry forward"
date: 2026-11-26
---

I looked into how [Inkling 2 Mini](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/) repeats its middle layers. Along the way, I reviewed what mixture-of-experts models and pruning do.

I also looked at two ways to reuse hidden states. [LoopRes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31094) lets a layer attend to outputs from earlier passes through the loop, while [Attention Latent Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31154) lets the current token draw on deeper layers from the previous token.
