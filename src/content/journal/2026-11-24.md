I went back to WaveFront Decoding and LoopCache to see how they approach decoding in looped Transformers. I also looked into GLM-5.4’s loop architecture.

For on-device inference, I wondered if offloading weights one layer at a time could use enough shared memory bandwidth to make the phone feel sluggish. I also wondered how much the hidden states change when the same layer runs again, and whether I could reuse the KV cache across passes.
